# Clear Writing

Write for the configured reader and purpose. Lead with the answer. Use concrete verbs, short paragraphs, specific nouns and only necessary technical terms. Remove inflated claims, empty transitions and repeated conclusions. Use tables for exact comparisons, diagrams for relationships, computed charts for numbers. Preserve names, quantities, units, negation, conditions, uncertainty and source citations during editing. Never invent anecdotes to sound human. A style lint pass does not establish factual accuracy. Submit an outcome for independent review; keep limitations visible.

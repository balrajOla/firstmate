# Brainstorm

Establish the intended outcome, reader, constraints, exclusions and evidence available. Retrieve relevant context; reopen decisive originals. Separate facts, assumptions, conflicts and unknowns. Compare at least two viable approaches when the decision merits it. Recommend one with its tradeoffs. Draw a small architecture diagram if topology matters. Ask only consequential unresolved questions. Produce a concise decision and testable acceptance criteria. This built-in procedure is not a claim that upstream Superpowers ran.
